About Me

I am a Machine Learning Engineer at TikTok (ByteDance) and an AI researcher with a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Stony Brook University, where I was advised by Prof. Zhaozheng Yin. My work spans AI agents, LLM/MLLM post-training, multimodal video understanding, and generative AI. During my Ph.D., I studied video-language models, video understanding, temporal grounding and retrieval, and biomedical image analysis.
